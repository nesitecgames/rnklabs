How to Merge PDF Files on Android

Combine two or more PDFs into one document with Merge. Arrange the input files in the intended reading order before saving.

By RNK Labs · October 30, 2026 · 4 steps

Screenshots show the English app interface. Translated button names may differ from the labels in the images.

THE APP USED IN THIS GUIDE

PDF Converter, Scanner & Tools

We’ll use RNK Labs’ PDF Converter, Scanner & Tools for Android throughout this tutorial. Install the app, then follow the steps below on your phone.

Get PDF Converter on Google Play ↗

Before you begin

Prepare at least two PDFs. Our examples use demo files. The order of selected documents determines the reading sequence in the merged PDF. The app screenshots use demo content with no personal data. Android file-picker labels may vary by device and language.

  1. 1. Open Merge
  2. 2. Arrange the input files
  3. 3. Save the combined document
  4. 4. Review the merged PDF

STEP 01 / 04

Open Merge

From Tools, tap Merge. Tap Select PDFs to open Android’s file picker. Select at least two PDF documents and confirm your selection.

STEP 02 / 04

Arrange the input files

Review the selected PDFs and their order. Use the available ordering controls to put the files in the sequence you want. Remove an unwanted file before continuing. For example, put the cover document before the main report.

STEP 03 / 04

Save the combined document

Tap Save PDF, choose a destination and give the combined file a recognizable name. Confirm the save and wait for completion.

STEP 04 / 04

Review the merged PDF

Open the output. Check the document boundaries, page order and total page count. If individual pages need to move after merging, open the result in Arrange and Rotate Pages.

Tips for a better result

  • Give source files recognizable names to avoid selecting the wrong version.
  • Keep the original PDFs until you have checked the merged output.
